## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an elevated holiday culinary experience for mobile-first café ordering. The narrative merges the warmth and quiet craftsmanship of an artisanal Italian roastery and patisserie with the timeless nostalgia of Christmas. Rather than leaning on overt commercial tropes (such as saturated greens or cartoonish ornaments), the tone is deeply refined, sensory, and intimate—evoking the aroma of freshly roasted beans, spiced brioche, mulled wine, and gilded festive accents.

The design movement blends **Warm Editorial Minimalist** with **Tactile Heritage Luxury**. Clean structural grids support high-density culinary menus, softened by creamy backgrounds, micro-tonal borders, and tactile accents that convey indulgence and celebratory care.

## Colors
The palette balances deep holiday warmth and rich coffee heritage:

- **Primary (`#6B1D2F` / `#80182A`):** Deep mulled wine / burgundy. Applied to key interactive triggers, promotional labels, prices, and header accent typography.
- **Secondary (`#1C3A27`):** Muted pine green. Evokes winter foliage and serves as a balanced secondary tone for dietary chips, specialty collection badges, and seasonal tags.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059` / `#DFBA73`):** Warm champagne gold. Imparts festive luxury to border highlights, subtle micro-accents, star ratings, and celebratory badges.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - `Canvas / Surface`: Cream milk foam (`#FDFBF7`).
  - `Surface Subdued / Cards`: Warm latte cream (`#F5EFEB`).
  - `Surface Outlines`: Soft hazelnut glaze (`#E7DEC8`).
  - `Text Primary`: Deep roasted espresso bean (`#3E2723`).
  - `Text Muted`: Steamed cinnamon taupe (`#7A6A65`).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans provides a clean, contemporary rhythm with warm, inviting geometric shapes that ensure high legibility on mobile screens. 

- **Section Titles:** Rendered in `headline-md` using deep espresso (`#3E2723`) with high visual contrast to anchor the menu structure.
- **Product Titles:** Set in `headline-sm` with tight leading, preventing tall vertical consumption in 2-column mobile grids.
- **Product Descriptions:** Rendered in `body-sm` using taupe (`#7A6A65`), restricted to a strict 2-line clamp.
- **Prices & Currency:** Prominently featured using `price-tag` in rich burgundy (`#6B1D2F`) for instant visual scanning.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a mobile-first 2-column fluid grid tailored for visual menu browsing:

- **Columns & Breakpoints:** Base layout spans 2 columns with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) lateral screen margins. Elements scale predictably up to a max-width container of 440px on hand-held devices and tablets.
- **Card Aspect Ratios:** Product imagery retains a consistent 4:3 or 1:1 square crop to maintain harmony across irregular food photography.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Section headers are padded with `space-xl` top margin and `space-sm` bottom margin to create distinct culinary stages (e.g., *Especiales*, *Clásicos*, *Café Fríos*).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through layered warm surfaces combined with warm, tinted micro-shadows:

- **Surface 0 (Canvas):** Pure porcelain latte cream (`#FDFBF7`).
- **Surface 1 (Menu Cards):** Warm milk-washed surface (`#FFFFFF` or `#FCFAF6`) bounded by a subtle `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.22)` gold-tinted hairline stroke.
- **Shadow Profile:** Micro-ambient elevation using espresso/wine diffusion rather than cold grey: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Floating Overlays:** Floating category navigation pills and cart buttons utilize a slightly deeper shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.14)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` provides friendly, artisanal curves that soften the interface without looking playful or cartoonish:

- **Cards:** Defined with `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) across top corners and base corners.
- **Pills & Badges:** Use full circular radius (`9999px`) for seasonal and dietary indicators.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** Shaped with `rounded-lg` (1rem) to mirror card boundaries.
- **Action Buttons & Modals:** Standardized with `0.75rem` to `1rem` corner rounding.

## Components

### Product Cards
- Built as vertically stacked containers on Surface 1.
- Top section contains the product image with top-left floating badges (e.g., "Navideño", "Sin TACC", "Favorito").
- Card body features:
  - Product title in `headline-sm` (`#3E2723`).
  - Secondary description in `body-sm` (`#7A6A65`) with line-clamp 2.
  - Price indicator in `price-tag` (`#6B1D2F`).
  - Subtle champagne border hairline with soft micro-shadow.

### Festive Badges & Chips
- **Seasonal Specials:** Burgundy fill (`#6B1D2F`) with ivory text (`#FDFBF7`) and tiny champagne icon accents.
- **Dietary (e.g., Sin TACC):** Pine green fill (`#1C3A27`) with soft cream text (`#FDFBF7`).
- **Category Filter Pills:** Ghost styling with warm cream background (`#F5EFEB`), dark espresso text, switching to solid burgundy (`#6B1D2F`) on active selection.

### Search Bar & Filters
- Set on a subtle inset container background (`#F5EFEB`) with a hairline border (`#E7DEC8`).
- Left-aligned magnifying icon in warm cinnamon taupe, accompanied by a trailing filter icon button.

### Primary Action Buttons
- High-contrast burgundy background (`#6B1D2F`) with warm gold micro-focus ring and cream typography (`#FDFBF7`).
- Subtle press feedback: gentle downscale (`transform: scale(0.98)`).

### Sticky Festive Footer & Cart Trigger
- Grounded in rich espresso brown (`#3E2723`) or burgundy (`#6B1D2F`) with champagne gold border trims (`#C5A059`).
- Contains store details, operating hours, and prominent social/contact handles.
